# 🌍 Today in Emojis

**Today’s vibe — in five emojis.**  
*(Feel the day. Don’t read it.)*

---

## Manifesto

> The world no longer asks for your opinion — it asks for your attention.  
> Every scroll, every click, every play is a toll on your time.  
>
> **Today in Emojis** is a quiet refusal.  
> A daily vibe-check of the planet — distilled into five tiny symbols.  
> No words. No headlines. No ads.  
> Just emotion, felt instantly.  
>
> Because you don’t owe the world your time.  
> But you can still sense its pulse.

---

## Positioning

**Today in Emojis** is a minimalist, visual experiment that captures the world’s collective mood in **five emojis each day**.  
It invites you to *feel* the news before (or instead of) reading it — a reminder that awareness doesn’t have to mean overload.

**Primary tagline (site):** “Today’s vibe — in five emojis.”  
**Secondary tagline (docs/social):** “Feel the day. Don’t read it.”

---

## Experience Principles

- **Instant emotion:** Page loads → five emojis appear instantly.  
- **No scroll:** Everything fits above the fold on desktop and mobile.  
- **No text clutter:** Title • five emojis • footer with reactions and links.  
- **Zero-cost stack:** Free-tier hosting, automation, and data sources.  
- **Playful closure:** Footer reactions (❤️ 🤔 👎) with instant feedback.  
- **Open-source:** Public repo, tiny link to README + GitHub in footer.

---

## MVP Scope (Phase 0 → v1)

- One-page site with: title, 5 emojis, footer (reactions + GitHub/README).  
- Emojis optionally link to representative news stories (click if you must).  
- Reactions stored locally first (progressive enhancement later).  
- Daily refresh (manual JSON at first; automation later).
